Comparative Design and Testing of Jet-Noise Suppression Nozzles

Overview Research Questions Methods Findings Conclusion References

Research Overview

Jet aircraft produce noise due to violent missing of exhaust gas and surrounding air, producing high frequency noise near the nozzle but low frequency downstream. This excessive noise can disturb nearby communities and affect quality of life.

This project will test and compare methods of jet-noise suppression to determine which design provides the best noise reduction while also maintaining acceptable exhaust-flow and engine-performance characteristics.

This website provides an overview of the research, including the research questions, methodology, findings, and relevant academic sources.
